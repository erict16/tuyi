Tuyi is installed on my computer. Please use it to translate the DWG / DXF drawings in my Drawings folder into English, save them as new files, and leave the originals alone.

The assistant has to be allowed to run programs on your computer. That usually means the desktop app, and it will ask you first.

Notes for developers

  • The original stays as it is

    The translation is written into a new file. Title blocks, notes, block attributes, dimensions and tables all get translated.

  • Trade terms come from a glossary

    Common building, switchgear and transformer terms are built in and work offline. You can add your own.

  • Many drawings at once

    Add several drawings, click 一起译 (translate together), and each one gets its own new file.

Free, no sign-up. Lines the glossary doesn't cover go to your own DeepL or Azure key.
